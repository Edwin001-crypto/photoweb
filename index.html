School Newsletter - School News

School News

Learning and Achievement

Our pupils have made an excellent start to the term. Teachers have been impressed by their enthusiasm, creativity and positive attitude towards learning.

Reading Challenge

This term we are encouraging every child to read regularly at home. Children can record the books they have read in their reading journals.

  • Read for at least 15 minutes each day.
  • Choose books that are interesting and challenging.
  • Talk about stories and new vocabulary at home.

Eco Club

The Eco Club has started a recycling project around the school. Pupils will learn how to reduce waste and care for our environment.

Congratulations!

Congratulations to our pupils who represented the school in the inter-school spelling competition. We are very proud of you!
